Shown in the logo strip under the hero (`js/data.js` → `logos`) and on the
Jeeb / Pass cards, to identify companies Afaque worked for and products he
helped build. Logos are trademarks of their owners; no endorsement is implied.
Downloaded 15 Oct 2026 from each company's official website (Jeeb and Pass
via Wayback Machine snapshots of their official sites, which are offline now).
Files were trimmed and re-encoded only — the marks themselves are unchanged.
